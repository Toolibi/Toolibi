An AI assistant for cabinet shops: give it a cabinet schedule and it reviews the list, builds the cut list, nests parts for the CNC router, prices the job and checks inventory. A 24-room hospital job (1,632 parts) runs in 3.7 seconds, and a supervisor approves every step.
